Components · three treatments, owner to pick (ticket 0072)

Wave ETA corner card

Fixed bottom-right of the map beside the right Panels, 300 px wide, the number at 120 px mono with the town as its label, inside the violet dashed Community edge (D-24). White, amber under 15 min, red under 5, “wave expected now” in red (D-25); it borrows only amber and red (D-54). The owner asked for it to read as critical danger from the back of a room; these are three ways, each shown in its four states. Nothing here is chosen.

A · Beacon

The card as the board drew it (D-24): violet dashed edge, number at 120 px with one soft text shadow in its own colour. Motion: the number breathes (opacity 1 to 0.72, 2 s) under 15 min; under 5 the whole edge pulses like the Takeover border. Only transform and opacity move. Quietest of the three; reads as the Community colour first and the danger colour second.

B · Tinted hazard

The card's fill takes the countdown colour at 12 % like the band, and the dashed edge thickens to 3 px and turns amber, then red. Motion: the number scales 1.03 to 1 on every minute tick (transform); under 5 the card nudges 2 px left and right once a second. Loudest; the colour fills a 300 px block, so it reads from the back of a room, but a red box may compete with an Official card.

C · Depletion rail

A rail under the number drains from the warning's full window (44 min at Betrawati) to zero, so the shrinking bar carries the urgency even when the number is not read. Motion: the rail's fill is a scaleX transform; under 5 it blinks at 1 Hz (opacity). Tallest card (about 150 px). Judges see time running out, not only a number.

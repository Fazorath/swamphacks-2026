Components · variant C (D-36, D-37, ticket 0070)

God's Eye View · Readings Panel, river ladder

The Readings Panel for a network of about 45 Nodes: the breadboard Pi pinned at the top with its cyan LIVE SENSORS tag, then the valley Nodes on a vertical river line in River km order with the wave front marked between the rungs it is passing, and the real Nodes off the river under a divider. A fleet Node never enters the ladder; clicked on the map it opens a popover beside its dot (D-38). 288 px wide; on the map it scrolls, here it is shown at full height.

Three states

Order never changes on its own. A rung flashes once when its risk rises, on the same frame as its marker's ring (D-13). A Stale rung says STALE with its age and strikes its last water level through (D-15).
